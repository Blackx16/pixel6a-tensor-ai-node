## 2. Why Larger Models Were Excluded for the 6 GB RAM Pixel 6a

* **Gemma 3n E4B IT int4 (4.41 GB disk)** and **Gemma 2 2B IT q8 (2.71 GB disk)** require ~6.2–6.98 GB of peak RSS ([AI Edge Gallery Allowlist](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/gallery/blob/main/model_allowlist.json)), which exceeds the Pixel 6a's 6 GB physical RAM (~5.43 GB usable) and triggers Android 17's `MemoryLimiter:AnonSwap` killer ([Android 17 All-Apps Behavior Changes](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/17/behavior-changes-all)).
* **Phi-3.5-mini-instruct 3.8B INT4 ONNX (2.78 GB disk)** peaks at ~3.3–3.8 GB RSS ([Microsoft Phi-3.5 ONNX](https://huggingface.co/microsoft/Phi-3.5-mini-instruct-onnx/tree/main/cpu_and_mobile/cpu-int4-awq-block-128-acc-level-4)), leaving insufficient headroom when Android 17 system processes use ~2.5 GB.
* By selecting **Gemma 4 E2B IT (`gemma-4-E2B-it-gpu.litertlm`, ~820 MB active GPU RAM with `mmap`ed embeddings)** ([Gemma 4 E2B Card](https://huggingface.co/litert-community/gemma-4-E2B-it-litert-lm)) and **Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct INT4 (`~1.35 GB RAM`)** ([Qwen2.5 Mobile Card](https://huggingface.co/pdnhan/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-mobile/tree/main/onnx)), both models run smoothly within the Pixel 6a's thermal and memory envelope.
